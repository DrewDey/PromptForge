Neon Block Patrol
v3 readability build

Open-city foam tag patrol.

Arcade-only browser prototype. Bright city blocks, cartoon mascots, giant checkpoint beams, clear hit flashes, and a fixed pre-start HUD showing the correct target count before play begins.

Opening view is intentional The first mascots and checkpoint beacon are placed directly ahead so the readability upgrades are obvious immediately.
Clear hit feedback Successful foam tags trigger a green crosshair pulse, floating score text, a sound pop, and a visible burst on the mascot.
Safe arcade tone No gore, no realistic crime tasks, no real-world weapon behavior. This is neon carnival target-tag gameplay.
Controls WASD to move, mouse to look, click to tag, Shift to sprint. Click start to enable pointer-lock aiming.
Browser note. Pointer lock needs a click. Audio feedback starts after the first user interaction.
