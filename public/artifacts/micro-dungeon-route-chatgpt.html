Controls

Tap or click adjacent rooms to sketch. Keyboard users can use Arrow Keys to add route steps, Backspace to undo, Enter to run, and Esc to reset to only Start.

Rules

  1. Routes move only up, down, left, or right.
  2. Keys add to your key count.
  3. Locked doors consume one key.
  4. Traps cost one health.
  5. Cracked floors collapse after you leave them.
  6. You win only if the route reaches the exit alive.
