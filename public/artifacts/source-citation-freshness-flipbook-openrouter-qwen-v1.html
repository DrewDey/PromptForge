How Risk is Calculated: Total Risk Score $R = A + E$, where $A$ is the Age Penalty ($0$ if $\le 2$ years, $+1$ if $2-5$ years, $+2$ if $>5$ years) and $E$ is the Evidence Penalty (Meta-analysis $= 0$, RCT $= 1$, Observational $= 2$, Expert $= 3$, Anecdotal $= 4$). Risk levels: $R \le 1$ (Low), $2 \le R \le 3$ (Medium), $R \ge 4$ (High).
