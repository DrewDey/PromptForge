How Risk is Calculated (Click to Expand)

Total Risk Score is calculated as $R = A + E$, where:

  • $A$ (Age Penalty): $0$ if $\le 2$ years, $+1$ if $2-5$ years, $+2$ if $>5$ years.
  • $E$ (Evidence Penalty): Meta-analysis $= 0$, RCT $= 1$, Observational $= 2$, Expert $= 3$, Anecdotal $= 4$.

Risk Levels: $R \le 1$ (Low), $2 \le R \le 3$ (Medium), $R \ge 4$ (High).
